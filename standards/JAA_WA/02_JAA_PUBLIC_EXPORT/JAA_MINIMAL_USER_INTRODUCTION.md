# JAA Minimal User Introduction

Timestamp: 2026-05-10 07:21:35

## What this is

JAA is a simple local note-memory system.

It helps you write notes, preserve them, find them later, and continue your work from what you actually wrote.

## Why it exists

People forget.

Projects scatter.

Important thoughts get lost in old files, chats, emails, and folders.

JAA gives your notes a simple daily home and makes them easier to recover.

## The basic loop

1. WRITE Notes.
2. ATOMIZE Notes.
3. CALL Notes.
4. READ Notes.

## What the commands mean

WRITE Notes means: save this note.

ATOMIZE Notes means: make today's notes searchable.

CALL Notes means: find relevant prior notes.

READ Notes means: use selected notes as the context for continuation.

## Important boundary

JAA does not decide what is true.

JAA does not publish your notes.

JAA does not replace your judgment.

JAA helps preserve and recover what you wrote.

## First habit

When something matters, write it.

When the day ends, atomize it.

When you need continuity, call it.

## User maxim

Write simply.
Recover reliably.
Continue from what was actually written.

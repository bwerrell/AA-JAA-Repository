# JAA Boundaries and Non-Promises

Timestamp: 2026-05-10 07:26:46

## Purpose

Define what JAA does not promise or perform by default.

## Core boundary

JAA is a note-memory and continuity system.

It preserves, structures, searches, and helps continue from notes.

It does not decide truth, publish claims, adjudicate disputes, or replace the user's judgment.

## JAA does not promise

- To determine what is true.
- To determine what is lawful.
- To determine what should be published.
- To protect confidential information unless the user applies proper handling rules.
- To promote candidate atoms into canonical records automatically.
- To replace review by a responsible human.
- To eliminate the need for backups.
- To guarantee that all relevant notes will be found by a search.

## Candidate atom boundary

Candidate atoms are working memory aids.

They are not canonical records unless a separate promotion act says so.

Source notes remain authoritative unless a governed promotion changes that status.

## Publication boundary

JAA does not publish notes by default.

Any publication, sharing, or external reporting requires a separate intentional act.

## User responsibility

The user remains responsible for judgment, review, privacy, backup, and external use.

## Maxim

Preserve first.
Recover second.
Judge separately.
